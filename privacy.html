Privacy Policy

Ask someone / Vraag iemand · Last updated: 1 July 2026

English

Ask does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app works entirely on your device. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and makes no network connections.
  • Your language and appearance preferences are saved locally on your device only (in the system's standard settings storage). They never leave your device and are removed if you delete the app.
  • We use no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no third-party services.
  • Because the app collects nothing, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete beyond removing the app.
  • The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

If you have any questions about this policy, contact: martijnberkhoff@protonmail.com
