Ask — conversation cards

Cards for honest conversation. · Kaarten voor echte gesprekken.

English

Ask is a deck of conversation cards for slowing down with another person — a partner, a friend, a parent, even a stranger. Pick a theme, take turns drawing a card, and answer honestly. No accounts, no ads, no tracking — everything stays on your device.
